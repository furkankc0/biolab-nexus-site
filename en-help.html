Help

The questions people ask most often during installation and first use. Every page of the program also has a Help button that explains how to use that page.

Installation

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

Windows shows this warning for new programs that are not yet widely used. If you downloaded the file from this site and its verification code matches, click More info, then Run anyway. If your institution’s computers do not show that button, your IT department can install it.

How do I install the APK on Android?
  1. Download the APK file from this site on your phone.
  2. Tap the download notification or the file.
  3. If the phone says installs from this source are not allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source and go back.
  4. Tap Install. You can turn the permission off again afterwards.

Menu names vary slightly between phone brands.

How do I install it on an iPhone?

Nothing is downloaded on an iPhone. Open your institution’s HMZ Lab address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The program opens full screen from its icon on the home screen.

Where do I find my institution’s HMZ Lab address?

Ask your lab administrator or teacher. The address is also in the link in your invitation email.

Does the program connect to the internet?

A program installed on a single computer works without internet and verifies its licence offline. When it is installed on a server, phones and computers connect to that server. In no case does the program send institutional data to the developer.

Account and sign-in

I don’t have an account. How do I get one?

Ask your teacher. The teacher enters your name and school email; once the administrator approves, a link arrives in your inbox. Open it and choose your own username and password.

The invitation email did not arrive

Check your spam folder first. The link is valid for 7 days; if it has expired or never arrived, ask your teacher or administrator for a new one.

Why are only school email addresses accepted?

To make sure accounts really belong to people at the institution. The administrator can open an account for a visiting researcher from outside by giving a reason.

I forgot my password

You can sign in with either your username or your email. If you can’t remember your password, ask your teacher or administrator for a password reset link; it arrives by email. Passwords are never sent by email.

Using the program

Does the program make the identification decision?

No. HMZ Lab explains the 16S result and its level of confidence and points out what to watch for; the expert makes the decision. Results depend on the reference version used and on the validation studies you run in your lab.

The program opens “read-only”

The licence may not be loaded, may have expired, or the program files may have been changed. You can still view, export and back up your records; no data is deleted. Contact your lab administrator.

How do I back up my data?

The program takes a backup automatically every day. You can also download all records as a single file from the backup section of the settings page.

How do I choose the light or dark theme?

On a computer, use the Theme box in the top bar; on a phone, at the bottom of the Menu. “System” follows your device’s setting.

How do I switch the program to English or Turkish?

Use the Language box in the top bar on a computer, or at the bottom of the Menu on a phone. The choice is remembered on that device; each person can use the language they prefer. Your records are not translated: names, notes and other text you enter stay as you wrote them.

Still stuck?

Email us with the problem, the device you are using and the message you see. If an error message shows a “kayıt no” (record number), include it as well.
