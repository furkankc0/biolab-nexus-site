From the plate to the 16S result, every lab record in one program

HMZ Lab brings together 16S identification from Sanger reads, zone measurements, experiment records and instrument bookings. Students, teachers and technicians reach the same records from a computer or a phone.

DownloadFor your institution

Windows, Android and iPhone. In English and Turkish.

What does it do in the lab?

DNA analysis and 16S identification

Trims .ab1 chromatograms from the sequencing facility by quality, builds a consensus from forward and reverse reads and searches the NCBI 16S reference. It explains the result with its level of confidence and the points to watch, and leaves the identification decision to the expert.

  • Chromatogram, FASTA file or pasted sequence
  • Batch identification of dozens of isolates
  • A second opinion from a second classifier using the same reference

Experiments and zone measurements

Records the zone diameters you measure in disc diffusion and well diffusion experiments, with biological and technical replicates. You can measure to scale on a plate photo and compare the result with published data.

  • Step-by-step experiment record, controls and photo attachment
  • Measurement in mm from a photo, using a scale line
  • Literature cards and method comparison

Instruments and bookings

Keeps the lab’s instrument inventory and booking calendar. A busy slot cannot be booked twice; maintenance and repair times appear on the calendar as blocks.

  • Daily and weekly plan per instrument
  • Maintenance and usage blocks
  • “Today” and “Tomorrow” summary on the home page

Validation

Measures and reports the program’s own identification accuracy: leave-one-out validation against the reference, a threshold study and comparison with real isolates. The results can serve as evidence in a thesis or report.

  • Leave-one-out (LOO) validation
  • Similarity threshold study
  • Comparison with the laboratory’s identification

Molecular analysis and 3D structures

Examines DNA, RNA and protein sequences. Fetches sourced protein cards from UniProt and 3D structures from the PDB; you can rotate and zoom a structure and focus on a residue.

  • GC content, reverse complement and six-frame translation
  • Protein cards stored together with their source
  • Rotatable 3D structure viewer, PNG download

Projects, samples and isolates

Organises records as projects, samples and isolates and links DNA analyses and measurements to them. Records with the same name never get mixed up, and every change is kept with its history.

  • Project, sample and isolate tree
  • Linking analyses and measurements to an isolate
  • Archiving and record history

Backup and security

Backs up all records every day at the time you choose and verifies the backup; keeps daily, weekly and monthly backups. The whole workspace can be downloaded as a single file and moved to another computer.

  • Automatic daily backup and verification
  • Single-file backup and restore
  • Raw records and an audit log that cannot be altered

Its own window on a computer, an app on a phone

On a computer the program opens in its own window, not in a browser tab. On a phone the tabs at the bottom reach the most common tasks in one tap, and “Menu” opens every section, including the 3D structure viewer. There are light and dark themes.

Install on your device

How to get started

  1. Setup

    We install the program at an address of your own, on our server or on your institution’s server. It also runs on a single lab computer, without internet.

  2. Accounts

    The administrator adds the teachers. A teacher enters a student’s name and school email; once the administrator approves, the student receives a link and chooses a username and password.

  3. Use

    Install the program on computers and the app on Android phones; on an iPhone, add it to the home screen from Safari. Everyone works on the same records, within their own permissions.

Your data stays with your institution

Laboratory records belong to the institution. The program never sends them to the developer and cannot be switched off remotely; even when a licence expires, records are not deleted and can still be viewed and exported.

  • Each institution’s program runs separately; one institution cannot see another’s users or records.
  • Raw records and the audit log cannot be altered afterwards; every record is checked with a SHA-256 digest.
  • A backup is taken and verified automatically every day.
  • Passwords are never sent by email; sign-in links work only once.
  • This site collects no data: no cookies, no tracking code and no forms.
